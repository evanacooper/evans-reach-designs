Engage · AI Builder

Build with AI — versions

Two takes on the AI-driven automations, broadcasts & segments builder. V1 is the leaner MVP that leans on today's product surfaces; V2 is the fuller redesign with an in-pane segment and a rich review card.

V1 · MVP
Leaner MVP — reuses today's surfaces
Same entry, plan-mode chat and create menu as V2, but simplified after the plan: a minimal inline loading indicator in the chat, a stripped-down "automation created" message with a small segment chip, a segment modal built on the partner-dashboard condition builder, and a standard steps + email-preview automation view when the AI chat is closed.
Inline loading Segment chip Segment modal
V2 · Full redesign
Full redesign — in-pane segment & review card
The richer take: one shell with a swappable left pane (automation ⇄ segment) and a single persistent chat, a "Who this goes to" review card that collapses on confirm, build success & failure paths, and the existing broadcast wizard. Every AI-built thing stays editable by hand.
